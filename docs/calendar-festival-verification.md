# Calendar festival verification

The regional catalog has six festival rules per tradition. The audit
evaluated every civil date from 2026 through 2036 for the three reference cities.
Each rule produced exactly one date every year in its reference city. The 2026
calendar is the baseline and 2027–2036 is the ten-year forecast.

| Tradition and reference city | Catalog | 2026 baseline | 2027–2031 | 2032–2036 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tamil · Solar, Chennai | Thai Pongal, Puthandu, Deepavali, Aadi Perukku, Rama Navami, Vinayagar Chathurthi | 6/6 exact | 30/30 exact | Supplementary same-city and regional comparisons |
| Telugu · Amavasyant, Tirupati | Ugadi, Vijayadashami, Deepavali, Sri Rama Navami, Raksha Bandhan, Vinayaka Chavithi | 6/6 exact | 30/30 exact | Supplementary national and regional comparisons |
| Ujjain tradition · Purnimant, Ujjain | Chaitra Navratri Begins, Vijayadashami, Diwali, Ram Navami, Raksha Bandhan, Ganesh Chaturthi | 6/6 exact | 30/30 exact | Supplementary national and Ujjain comparisons |

Exact matches in 2026–2031 use annual Drik Panchang calendars configured for
[Chennai](https://www.drikpanchang.com/tamil/tamil-calendar.html?geoname-id=1264527&year=2027),
[Tirupati](https://www.drikpanchang.com/telugu/calendar/telugu-calendar.html?geoname-id=1254360&year=2027)
and [Ujjain](https://www.drikpanchang.com/calendars/hindu/hinducalendar.html?geoname-id=1253914&year=2027).
The same annual URLs did not return the longer-range editions during the audit.

## Aadi Perukku and ritual-window festivals

| Year | Aadi Perukku, Chennai | Rama Navami, three reference cities | Raksha Bandhan, Tirupati and Ujjain | Ganesh Chaturthi, three reference cities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2032 | Aug 2 | Apr 18 | Aug 20 | Sep 8 |
| 2033 | Aug 3 | Apr 7 | Aug 10 | Aug 28 |
| 2034 | Aug 3 | Mar 28 | Aug 29 | Sep 16 |
| 2035 | Aug 3 | Apr 16 | Aug 18 | Sep 5 |
| 2036 | Aug 2 | Apr 5 | Aug 6 | Aug 24 |

- Aadi Perukku uses Aadi 18 under the Tamil solar calculation. The earlier calculation
  placed the 2033 Karka ingress at about 6:35 p.m. IST. The independent Swiss
  Ephemeris 2.10 Lahiri calculation puts it at about 6:44 p.m., after sunset
  in both Chennai and Madurai. Aadi 1 is Jul 17 and Aadi Perukku is Aug 3.
  The updated Tamil calculation matched all 264 independently computed month
  starts in those cities from 2026 through 2036. Swiss Ephemeris was used
  for verification only. In 2032 and 2036 the Aug 2 dates match the Chennai
  Thirukanitha variant. [Swiss Ephemeris](https://www.astro.com/ftp/swisseph/doc/swephprg.2.10.pdf) ·
  [Chennai Aadi Perukku by variant](https://nammacalendar.com/en/festival/aadi-perukku/2026/)
- Rama Navami and Ganesh Chaturthi compare the exact tithi overlap with local
  madhyahna, the middle fifth of daylight. The 2029 Rama Navami result is
  Apr 22 in Ujjain and Apr 23 in Tirupati and Chennai. In 2032 the AstroSage
  national calendar lists Apr 19. Chandra uses Apr 18, which the Chennai
  forecast and the published Smarta date corroborate. [Chennai Rama Navami](https://udaya.app/festival/ramanavami/) ·
  [2032 Smarta Rama Navami](https://panchangtime.com/festivals/rama-navami-smarta/yearly/2032) ·
  [Ujjain Ganesh Chaturthi](https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/ganesh-chaturthi/ganesh-chaturthi-date-time.html?geoname-id=1253914)
- Raksha Bandhan uses Bhadra-free Shravana Purnima in aparahna or pradosh.
  When those windows are unavailable, the next morning qualifies if Purnima
  lasts three muhurtas from sunrise. The 2026 case qualifies for Aug 28.
  In Tirupati in 2031, Purnima ends about 79 minutes after sunrise, so the
  earlier night yields Aug 2. [Dharmasindhu rule discussed in Utsav Nirnaya](https://egyankosh.ac.in/bitstream/123456789/80293/1/Block-4.pdf)

## Events and recurring observances

Events, Calendar and Panchang use the saved display tradition and the calculation city. The Sankatahara or Sankashti Chaturthi filter shows the waning observance. The annual Ganesh festival appears under Festivals. Astronomical events are global solar and lunar eclipses assigned to the IST date of peak.

- Tirupati's Padmini Ekadashi falls on May 27, 2026, after a repeated sunrise Tithi. Devutthana falls on November 20, when sunrise shows Dashami. Parama Ekadashi falls on June 11 in the same Adhik Masa. The shared observance rule selects the Smarta household date. [Tirupati Telugu calendar](https://www.drikpanchang.com/telugu/calendar/telugu-calendar.html?geoname-id=1254360) · [Ekadashi rule examples](https://dharmagya.app/docs/panchang/festivals)
- Bengaluru shows Dhanteras and Pradosh Vrat on November 6, 2026. Sunrise shows Dwadashi and Trayodashi overlaps the evening window. Sankatahara Chaturthi checks local moonrise. [Pradosham rule](https://www.drikpanchang.com/vrats/pradoshdates.html?geoname-id=1278100) · [Sankashti rule](https://www.drikpanchang.com/vrats/sankashti-chaturthi-dates.html?geoname-id=1264527)
- Tamil Sashti is monthly and prefers a Panchami-to-Sashti transition before local sunset. Telugu Subrahmanya Shashti is the annual Margashirsha celebration. The Telugu Kalabhairava Ashtami filter includes monthly Kalashtami and the annual Kartika Jayanti. [Chennai Sashti dates](https://www.drikpanchang.com/vrats/skanda-sashti-dates.html?geoname-id=1264527&year=2026) · [Kalabhairava and Kalashtami dates](https://www.drikpanchang.com/vrats/masik-kalashtami-dates.html?time-format=24hour)
- Tamil Krithigai uses the first local sunset during Krittika Nakshatra. When the transit falls between sunsets, the app uses the sunrise in Krittika. [Krithigai observance](https://www.drikpanchang.com/vrats/masik-karthigai-dates.html) · [Calendar calculation convention](https://dharmagya.app/docs/panchang-ts/accuracy)
- Ujjain’s Vinayaka Chaturti uses waxing Chaturthi during local midday. The Bhadrapada occurrence appears as the annual Ganesh festival. Masik Durgashtami uses waxing Ashtami at sunrise. Ujjain’s November 2026 dates are November 13 and 17, respectively. [Ujjain November 2026 calendar](https://www.realpanchang.in/en/panchang/ujjain/2026/11) · [Monthly Durgashtami observance](https://www.drikpanchang.com/vrats/masik-durgashtami-dates.html)
- Moon phases lists the locally named Purnima and Amavasya observances in every tradition. Each observance uses the first local sunrise during the tithi. A tithi that falls entirely between sunrises uses the day it begins after sunrise. The same fallback applies to Ujjain’s Masik Durgashtami.
- When Trayodashi misses both sunset windows, the app uses the later date, as it does in Bengaluru on November 4, 2029. Sankashti also uses the later date when both moonrises miss Chaturthi. [Pradosh date rules](https://epandit.in/en/learn/festival/pradosh-vrat) · [Sankashti date rules](https://epandit.in/en/learn/festival/sankashti-chaturthi)
- The regression suite covers regional dates from 2026 through 2030, including Chennai Vinayagar Chathurthi on September 4, 2027, Ujjain Diwali on October 17, 2028, and Chennai Deepavali on October 26, 2030. It also checks that the shared Makar Sankranti entry falls on January 15, 2027, in Bengaluru after the previous day's post-sunset ingress. [Chennai 2027](https://www.drikpanchang.com/tamil/tamil-calendar.html?geoname-id=1264527&year=2027) · [Ujjain Diwali](https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/lakshmipuja/festivals-lakshmipuja-timings.html?geoname-id=1253914) · [Chennai 2030](https://www.drikpanchang.com/tamil/tamil-calendar.html?geoname-id=1264527&year=2030)
