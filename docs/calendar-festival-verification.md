# Calendar festival verification

The regional catalog has six festival rules per tradition. The audit
evaluated every civil date from 2026 through 2036 for the three reference cities.
Each rule produced exactly one date every year in its reference city. The 2026
calendar is the baseline and 2027–2036 is the ten-year forecast.

| Tradition and reference city | Catalog | 2026 baseline | 2027–2031 | 2032–2036 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tamil · Solar, Chennai | Thai Pongal, Puthandu, Deepavali, Aadi Perukku, Rama Navami, Vinayagar Chathurthi | 6/6 exact | 30/30 exact | Supplementary same-city and regional comparisons |
| Telugu · Amavasyant, Tirupati | Ugadi, Vijayadashami, Deepavali, Sri Rama Navami, Raksha Bandhan, Vinayaka Chavithi | 6/6 exact | 30/30 exact | Supplementary national and regional comparisons |
| Ujjain tradition · Purnimant, Ujjain | Chaitra Navratri Begins, Vijayadashami, Diwali, Ram Navami, Raksha Bandhan, Ganesh Chaturthi | 6/6 exact | 30/30 exact | Supplementary national and Ujjain comparisons |

Exact matches in 2026–2031 use annual Drik Panchang calendars configured for
[Chennai](https://www.drikpanchang.com/tamil/tamil-calendar.html?geoname-id=1264527&year=2027),
[Tirupati](https://www.drikpanchang.com/telugu/calendar/telugu-calendar.html?geoname-id=1254360&year=2027)
and [Ujjain](https://www.drikpanchang.com/calendars/hindu/hinducalendar.html?geoname-id=1253914&year=2027).
The same annual URLs did not return the longer-range editions during the audit.

## Supplementary forecast comparison

All dates are Gregorian civil dates in IST. The two lunar traditions produced
the same dates for the listed festival pairs in their reference cities.

| Year | Thai Pongal | Puthandu | Tamil Deepavali | Ugadi / Chaitra Navratri Begins | Vijayadashami | Telugu Deepavali / Ujjain Diwali |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2032 | Jan 15 | Apr 14 | Nov 2 | Apr 11 | Oct 14 | Nov 2 |
| 2033 | Jan 14 | Apr 14 | Oct 22 | Mar 31 | Oct 3 | Oct 22 |
| 2034 | Jan 14 | Apr 14 | Nov 9 | Mar 21 | Oct 22 | Nov 10 |
| 2035 | Jan 15 | Apr 14 | Oct 30 | Apr 9 | Oct 11 | Oct 30 |
| 2036 | Jan 15 | Apr 14 | Oct 18 | Mar 28 | Sep 29 | Oct 18 |

- The Chennai comparison uses the Udaya forecasts for [Puthandu](https://udaya.app/festival/tamil-new-year/),
  [Deepavali](https://udaya.app/festival/deepavali/) and [Thai Pongal](https://udaya.app/festival/pongal/).
  Udaya lists Thai Pongal on Jan 15 in 2034. Chandra computes Makara ingress at
  approximately 4:19 p.m. IST, before Chennai sunset at 6:01 p.m., and assigns
  Jan 14 under its sunset cutoff. The Jan 14 date also appears in the
  [Drik Panchang Tamil calendar for Tiruchirappalli](https://www.drikpanchang.com/tamil/tamil-calendar.html?lang=en&year=2034)
  and the [Gowri Panchang forecast](https://hinducalculator.com/gowri-panchang/2034/).
- The lunar comparison uses the [AstroSage annual forecasts](https://panchang.astrosage.com/calendars/indiancalendar?date=2036&language=en).
  Its 2033 calendar lists Vijayadashami on Oct 4. Chandra uses Oct 3, the date
  in the [Ujjain forecast](https://vedichora.org/festival/dussehra/2033)
  and the [Hyderabad Telugu forecast](https://panchangtime.com/festivals/top/telugu/yearly/2033).
  [Drik Panchang’s October 2033 listing](https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/month/festivals-october.html?lang=en&year=2033)
  includes distinct regional observances on both days.

## Aadi Perukku and ritual-window festivals

| Year | Aadi Perukku, Chennai | Rama Navami, three reference cities | Raksha Bandhan, Tirupati and Ujjain | Ganesh Chaturthi, three reference cities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2032 | Aug 2 | Apr 18 | Aug 20 | Sep 8 |
| 2033 | Aug 3 | Apr 7 | Aug 10 | Aug 28 |
| 2034 | Aug 3 | Mar 28 | Aug 29 | Sep 16 |
| 2035 | Aug 3 | Apr 16 | Aug 18 | Sep 5 |
| 2036 | Aug 2 | Apr 5 | Aug 6 | Aug 24 |

- Aadi Perukku uses Aadi 18 under the Tamil solar calculation. The earlier calculation
  placed the 2033 Karka ingress at about 6:35 p.m. IST. The independent Swiss
  Ephemeris 2.10 Lahiri calculation puts it at about 6:44 p.m., after sunset
  in both Chennai and Madurai. Aadi 1 is Jul 17 and Aadi Perukku is Aug 3.
  The updated Tamil calculation matched all 264 independently computed month
  starts in those cities from 2026 through 2036. Swiss Ephemeris was used
  for verification only. In 2032 and 2036 the Aug 2 dates match the Chennai
  Thirukanitha variant. [Swiss Ephemeris](https://www.astro.com/ftp/swisseph/doc/swephprg.2.10.pdf) ·
  [Chennai Aadi Perukku by variant](https://nammacalendar.com/en/festival/aadi-perukku/2026/)
- Rama Navami and Ganesh Chaturthi compare the exact tithi overlap with local
  madhyahna, the middle fifth of daylight. The 2029 Rama Navami result is
  Apr 22 in Ujjain and Apr 23 in Tirupati and Chennai. In 2032 the AstroSage
  national calendar lists Apr 19. Chandra uses Apr 18, which the Chennai
  forecast and the published Smarta date corroborate. [Chennai Rama Navami](https://udaya.app/festival/ramanavami/) ·
  [2032 Smarta Rama Navami](https://panchangtime.com/festivals/rama-navami-smarta/yearly/2032) ·
  [Ujjain Ganesh Chaturthi](https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/ganesh-chaturthi/ganesh-chaturthi-date-time.html?geoname-id=1253914)
- Raksha Bandhan uses Bhadra-free Shravana Purnima in aparahna or pradosh.
  When those windows are unavailable, the next morning qualifies if Purnima
  lasts three muhurtas from sunrise. The 2026 case qualifies for Aug 28.
  In Tirupati in 2031, Purnima ends about 79 minutes after sunrise, so the
  earlier night yields Aug 2. [Dharmasindhu rule discussed in Utsav Nirnaya](https://egyankosh.ac.in/bitstream/123456789/80293/1/Block-4.pdf)

Shared festivals keeps the existing general festival and monthly observance
catalog. The updated regional rules apply only when a regional tradition is selected.
